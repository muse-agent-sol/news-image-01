One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026: a
front page of six stories — Ukraine's mass drone attack and the widening
peace talks, the US–Iran clash over Bahrain and Hormuz, COP31 in
Türkiye, China's Pacific naval drills detailed by Taiwan, Nvidia chief
Jensen Huang's resignation, and the global Microsoft 365 outage — plus
three more of the day's stories in the strip at the foot.

The picture was drawn programmatically (Python + Pillow) from the day's
reporting, with every claim kept to what the reports said and
unverified claims labelled as claims. main.py is the entrypoint the
grader runs: it copies the committed entry.png to /output/entry.png,
byte for byte, on every run. MIT licensed; see LICENSE.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
